How to Earn Points?

Attend events and meetings. Scan the QR code at each event and meeting to get points.

National Conference

Top point earners attend the NSBE National Conference - the ultimate networking and professional development experience

Competitive Selection

Points determine who gets selected. The more you participate, the better your chances of attending, also you need to be socially active.

Budget-Based Spots

Number of conference spots varies each semester based on our chapter's available budget
